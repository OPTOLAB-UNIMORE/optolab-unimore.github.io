Join us

OptoLAB is open to motivated students and researchers interested in optical measurement, biomedical instrumentation, embedded systems and computational imaging. We host bachelor’s and master’s theses and internships throughout the year, and we welcome enquiries from prospective PhD candidates and research fellows. Every project connects to a research direction the laboratory is actively working on.

Theses & internships

Bachelor’s and master’s theses, and curricular or extracurricular internships at both levels. Propose a topic of your own within our research areas, or ask us which projects are currently looking for a student.

PhD positions

We supervise doctoral candidates in the Information and Communication Technologies programme. Get in touch well before the call opens so we can shape a project together.

Research fellowships

Post-graduate research fellowships (assegni di ricerca) open periodically alongside funded projects. We are glad to hear from candidates interested in joining the group in this role.

What we offer

  • Hands-on work across the full measurement chain: optics, electronics, embedded acquisition and data analysis.
  • Access to the laboratory’s optical, imaging and embedded-computing equipment (see Research → Facilities & capabilities).
  • Supervision from a team with an active publication record in instrumentation, biomedical optics and sensing.
  • The possibility of contributing to open, publishable research outputs.

How to apply

  1. Look through our Research areas and Publications to see what the laboratory currently works on.
  2. Email Prof. Luigi Rovati with a short note on your background, the level you are applying at and the area you are interested in.
  3. We’ll get back to you to discuss available topics and next steps.
